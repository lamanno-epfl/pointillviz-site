The spatial omics workbench

PointillViz

Visualize, explore and analyze millions of cells, molecules and modalities live in the browser, extended by a community tool store and wired to AI agents through MCP.

PointillViz is an interactive workbench for spatial omics that runs in the browser. It opens anything from a single tissue section to a cohort atlas of more than ten million cells, shows every cell, every detected molecule and every measured modality, and puts live exploration instruments, more than thirty analyses, a community tool store and an AI agent that works through PointillViz's own MCP tools on the same screen.

Developed in the La Manno Lab, EPFL
